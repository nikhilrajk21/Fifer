# Safety and privacy boundaries

This is a coursework prototype only. Use fictional scenarios and never real distress disclosures.

- Do not diagnose, claim therapeutic effectiveness, or imply human monitoring.
- Keep crisis access independent of any model or classifier.
- Do not automatically call or message emergency services.
- Keep the 5-4-3-2-1 skill sequence controlled by the application, not invented by a model.
- Keep user control, Stop/Leave, Pause, and Emergency Support available.
- Do not add real crisis phone numbers or emergency-contact integrations at this stage.
- Never place credentials in frontend code, reports, screenshots, prompts, or Git.
- Before adding dependencies, follow CHECK → TAKE ACTION → VERIFY and record actual evidence.
